SiddMiles

A private household credit-card, expense & rewards tracker

SiddMiles is a personal finance application built and used by two people — Siddharth and Neha — to keep track of their household's credit cards, spending, and rewards points in one place. It is not a public product, is not distributed to other users, and is not for sale.

What it does

  • Reads credit card statement PDFs and bank transaction-alert emails to build a unified record of spending across multiple cards and two people.
  • Tracks annual fees, billing/due dates, and reward-point balances so nothing is missed.
  • Shows a dashboard of spending by category, card, and time period.

Why it requests Gmail access

With explicit Google sign-in consent, the app requests read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) so it can automatically find bank statement and transaction-alert emails from the owners' own bank accounts, instead of requiring manual upload of every PDF. It only reads messages from a fixed list of known bank sender addresses — nothing else in either owner's inbox is accessed.
